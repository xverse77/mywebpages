I

Tulasī

Her leaf never wilts on his chest. Each bead of the mālā is a small vow — drag the circle, let a bead pass under your thumb, and it remembers a name.

III

Kṣīrasāgara

He sleeps on the coiled body of Śeṣa, afloat on an ocean of milk, so that the worlds resting on his breath can keep dreaming.

IV

Two hands

hold the conch and the mace — they keep the world standing.

V

The third and fourth

hold the lotus and the wheel — one gives, the other cuts through whatever needs cutting.
