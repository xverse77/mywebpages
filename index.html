Bleach : Thousand Year Blood War

Bleach: Thousand-Year Blood War is the final arc of the Bleach manga series, written and illustrated by Tite Kubo. It follows the story of Ichigo Kurosaki and his friends as they face off against the Wandenreich, a powerful group of Quincy warriors led by Yhwach. The arc explores themes of loyalty, sacrifice, and the consequences of war, culminating in an epic battle that determines the fate of the Soul Society and the world of the living.
